ABOUT VORSIGHT

The Think Tank with AI Capabilities.

Founded in Berlin, 2027

Vorsight started as a working group of researchers and risk practitioners who thought probability belonged closer to the decision, not buried three tabs deep in a spreadsheet nobody re-opens until the next audit.

We build the graph we always wished existed on the other side of the table — one that treats a risk estimate as a living number with a traceable history, not a static rating renewed once a year out of habit.

Built by software engineers who want to make the world more resilient.

WHERE THE TEAM COMES FROM

One team, pulled from very different rooms.

Academic research, capital markets, insurance, and reporting all have their own way of asking "how sure are we." We hired for the disagreement.
