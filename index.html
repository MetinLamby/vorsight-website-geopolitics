WHO IT IS FOR

Built for supply-chain-heavy industries.

We serve small and mid-sized European companies whose business depends on global supply chains. Here is what is active for your industry, and why it matters to you.

01 / 05Active threats

Chemicals & petrochemicals

Energy, feedstock and shipping lanes set your margins. Policy moves them first.

  • Gas and energy shocksSupply cuts and sanctions can change input costs within days.
  • Trade defence and tariffsAnti-dumping duties and retaliation reshape export markets.
  • Shipping chokepointsRed Sea and Gulf disruption delays feedstock and deliveries.
02 / 05Active threats

Automotive manufacturing

Electrification ties every carmaker and supplier to a few countries and a few materials.

  • Rare earth export licensingMagnets for motors and sensors can be held up at the border.
  • Tariffs on vehicles and partsUS and EU–China disputes change the price of every export.
  • Chip supply concentrationExport controls and tension around a few fabs.
03 / 05Active threats

Electronics, electrical engineering & semiconductors

Your products depend on technology and materials that governments now treat as strategic.

  • Chip export controlsRules on equipment and advanced chips change who can buy what.
  • Critical mineralsLicensing on gallium, germanium and graphite squeezes inputs.
  • Taiwan Strait riskAdvanced chip output is concentrated in one exposed place.
04 / 05Active threats

Food, beverage & FMCG

Thin margins and fast turnover leave no room for a blocked port or a new duty.

  • Shipping and canal disruptionRed Sea and Panama bottlenecks lift freight costs and delay stock.
  • Fertiliser and grain tradeBlack Sea tension and export bans move input prices.
  • Retaliatory tariffsAgri-food is the first target in trade disputes.
05 / 05Active threats

Pharmaceuticals & life sciences

A single missing ingredient or a delayed cold chain can stop a product line.

  • Active ingredient concentrationKey inputs come from a few countries that can restrict exports.
  • Tariffs on pharma importsNew duties reshape pricing and where you manufacture.
  • Logistics hub disruptionCold chains depend on a handful of air and sea hubs.
